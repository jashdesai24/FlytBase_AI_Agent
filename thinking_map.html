How We Deconstructed & Solved The Problem

A visual breakdown of our architectural thinking, core decisions, and engineering trade-offs to build a hallucination-free AI sales execution pipeline.

MAP 01 Core Problem vs. Engineering Architecture
1. The Challenges
PROBLEM 01

Manual Pre-Sales Fatigue

Researching target accounts, finding firmographic size, and identifying pain points takes an Account Executive 45+ minutes of repetitive manual browsing per inbound lead.

Goal: Reduce intelligence lookup time to under 15 seconds.
PROBLEM 02

LLM Score Hallucination

Asking an LLM prompt to "score this lead from 1 to 100" generates completely inconsistent, biased, and non-auditable scores that sales managers refuse to trust.

Goal: 100% explainable, rule-based lead qualification.
2. The Strategy
STRATEGY 01

LangGraph Multi-Agent DAG

We rejected messy monolithic prompts. We designed an orchestration graph of 7 micro-agents where each node enforces strict Pydantic typed schemas, preventing conversation drift.

Advantage: Modular, predictable data parsing.
STRATEGY 02

Simultaneous Parallel Processing

Sequential processing creates 30+ second delays. Notice that Account Research & Contact Intelligence don't depend on each other, so we engineered them to execute in parallel!

Advantage: Cuts total runtime almost in half.
3. Key Decisions
DECISION 01

Deterministic Lead Scoring

Agent 4 executes pure Python evaluation math (+25 ICP Enterprise Size, +20 C-Suite/VP seniority, +15 Corporate Domain, +20 Intent Signals). Zero AI guessing!

Outcome: Audit-ready letter grades (A through D).
DECISION 02

Smart Automated Branching

Leads scoring ≥ 40 advance to case study matching and AE briefing. Low-fit leads (< 40) automatically break out into automated nurture campaigns to preserve sales engineering time.

Outcome: AEs only spend time on qualified high-fit accounts.
4. Execution & Quality
DELIVERABLE 01

Vector RAG Case Study Hooks

Cold outreach without evidence is ignored. Agent 5 scans local ChromaDB embeddings of historical customer success stories, inserting relevant statistical proof points into emails.

Impact: Highly personalized 3-touch outbound cadences.
DELIVERABLE 02

Real-Time SSE Console & Reliability

Built asynchronous Server-Sent Events (SSE) streaming with anti-buffering proxy headers and sliding quota backoffs (4s/8s/15s) over verified 2026 Gemini Flash models.

Impact: Live token-by-token terminal observability without timeouts.
FLOW 02 End-to-End Pipeline Execution Timeline

How data evolves from a raw website form to an Account Executive sales briefing package.

01

Lead Intake & Normalization

Cleans free-text form inputs into a strict LeadRecord schema. Separates corporate addresses from freemail (.edu/gmail) and extracts urgency cues from user messages.

02

Parallel Web Research

Fires simultaneous branches: Agent 2 runs live Tavily search for company firmographics while Agent 3 builds a buyer seniority authority matrix.

03

Deterministic Qualification Edge

Agent 4 calculates an exact numerical score (0-100). Applies conditional graph routing (should_nurture_or_advance) based on ICP fit.

04

Case Study Vector Retrieval

Agent 5 performs semantic ChromaDB searches against historical FlytBase drone deployments to pair the prospect's industry with matching success stories.

05

3-Touch Cadence Generation

Agent 6 drafts an unyielding outbound sequence: Touch 1 (Pain Hypothesis), Touch 2 (Case Study Statistical Proof), and Touch 3 (Executive Check-in).

06

AE Executive Handoff Package

Agent 7 produces a comprehensive summary brief with discovery questions, qualification audit trails, and recommended talking points for sales leadership.
